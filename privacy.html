Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

This website is run by Petr Ilic, based in the Czech Republic, under the names The Balkan Life and The Balkan Renaissance. For anything about your data, write to thebalkanlife@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Form submissions. When you use the "Partner with us" or "Share your story" form, we receive what you type in: typically your name, email address, country or location, links to your website or social profiles, and any message or details you choose to add.
  • Technical data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page. We do not receive this in a usable form.
  • Video. The intro video comes from YouTube and loads only after you click it, using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com address. Before you click, nothing is requested from YouTube. After you click, Google may process data under its own policy.

We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not set cookies of our own. If we add visitor analytics later, we will choose a privacy-friendly tool and update this page first.

Why we use it

  • To read and reply to partner applications and interview sign-ups.
  • To decide whether a partnership or interview is a good fit, and to arrange it.
  • To keep the forms free of spam.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Form submissions are saved in a private Google Sheet through a Google Apps Script. Only we have access to that sheet. Google acts as our service provider and may process data in the United States and other countries under its standard safeguards.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We share it with a third party only if you ask us to, for example by agreeing to an introduction to a partner. If you join as an interview guest, we publish your name, story or business details only after you agree.

How long we keep it

We keep a submission for as long as the conversation or collaboration is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case it picks up again. We review the sheet regularly and delete entries we no longer need. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you,
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • limit how we use it,
  • give you a copy you can reuse, and
  • stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interest.

Email us and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz), or to the authority in your own country.

Other websites

This site links to other services, including our Skool community. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
